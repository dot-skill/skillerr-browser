Research folders

Your research, filed. On your own disk.

The first browser we know of that files your AI's research into real folders on your own disk.

As your AI files each session under topics, Skillerr turns them into a tree of folders, like Travel › Japan › Tokyo. Each holds the sessions, notes, skills and pages behind it, and links to related folders.

  • Real folders, real files. Each topic is a folder in ~/Skillerr/research with a Markdown index and your notes. Open it in Finder or Explorer, sync it, share it.
  • Linked folders. Topics that share research point to each other.
  • One click to pick it up again. Copy prompt for your AI puts a one-line hand-off on your clipboard. Paste it into any AI connected to Skillerr and it continues from what's there.
  • Show on map. Jump from any folder to its place in the graph.

Control

It does the clicking. You make the calls.

Every step shows up in plain words, and anything that spends money, signs in or deletes waits for you.

  • Pause stops it at once, with one button or ⇧⌘P. Take over at any moment.
  • Sensitive actions always ask, even with approvals off. The browser enforces it.
  • Robot checks are yours. When a page asks "are you human?", Skillerr hands it to you, then carries on.
  • Undo the last step: a mistyped field, a wrong choice, a wrong click.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. Connect Claude Desktop (the free plan works), Claude Code, Cursor, a local model or your own API key, and Skillerr costs nothing, with no features held back.

Where does my research graph live?

On your computer. Skillerr has no server that collects it. What leaves your computer is what your chosen AI reads to do the task: the pages it opens, and related past research it gets from recall. That goes to the AI you connected, or nowhere if it's a local model.

What does it remember, and how do I make it forget?

For each research session: the goal, which AI ran it, the pages visited (URL, title and a few keywords, never the full text), the notes and skills it produced, and its topics. Pages with password or payment fields are kept as a visit only. Use "Don't remember" on any session, Forget on any node, turn memory off, or clear everything in History & Bookmarks.

Can my AI pick up earlier research in a new chat?

Yes. Notes live in ~/Skillerr/notes, and Skillerr tells connected AIs where they are, offers them as MCP resources, and gives your AI my_research and read_note, so a new chat continues instead of starting over. Learned skills can also be shared with Claude Code.

Where do research folders live?

In ~/Skillerr/research on your computer: one folder per topic, each with a Markdown index and links to your notes (in ~/Skillerr/notes). They're ordinary files, so you can open, sync or share them.

Does my AI need Skillerr open?

To browse, yes; Skillerr starts by itself when your AI first uses it. To read your notes and skills, no: they're shared with your AI straight from disk.

Which AIs work?

Claude Desktop, Claude Code and Cursor connect in one click. Windsurf, Cline, LM Studio and other MCP apps connect with a config you copy. In the built-in panel: Ollama or LM Studio models, or a Gemini, Claude, OpenAI or OpenRouter key. ChatGPT isn't supported yet.

Can a website trick the AI?

It can try. Prompt injection is an open problem for every AI browser. Skillerr marks page text as untrusted, never lets payments, passwords, sign-ins or deletions through without your OK, and gives you Pause, Take over and Undo. Watching it work is part of the safety.

Our mission

Research that outlives the chat.

The web was built for people clicking one page at a time. Now AI agents browse it for us, and the old browser gets in the way: hidden searches you can't check, answers that vanish when the chat ends, and forty heavy tabs you never read but are scared to close.

Skillerr is a browser made for that new reader. Your AI does the reading in the open, in tabs you can watch and pause. What it learns is kept: a private research graph of every page, note and connection, so research compounds instead of evaporating. Our mission is simple: make AI research on the web visible, trustworthy, and cumulative, so every question you ask makes the next one easier.

Why “Skillerr”

A skill is something you only have to learn once. A skiller is the one who keeps learning them. When your AI works out how a site really works, like the hidden step or the fussy date picker, Skillerr saves it as a skill, with your OK, and uses it next time.
